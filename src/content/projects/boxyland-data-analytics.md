## Bringing data together

At BOXYLAND.EU, the company brought data from several countries together into one source. My work is to support and develop that foundation so the people using it can work with the data more easily.

## My contribution

- Maintain and develop the shared data source.
- Add new data sources as needs evolve.
- Work with Meltano for data integration and Metabase for analytics.
- Simplify the experience for the people working with the data.

## The result

The company can work with a unified source covering several countries. My ongoing contribution is to keep that resource useful, extend its coverage and make everyday analysis more approachable for its users.

## A continuing responsibility

Data work continues after the first integration. Maintaining sources and responding to user needs are an ongoing part of the role, connecting technical development with the needs of the people using the analytics.
