## My contribution

- Implemented object detection on Raspberry Pi with OpenCV and Hailo tooling.
- Connected target selection to servo aiming and search after losing a target.
- Developed Arduino control for three servomotors.
- Built a mobile interface for target selection, camera images and search control.
- Worked on YOLO training and model conversion, optimisation and compilation for Hailo.
