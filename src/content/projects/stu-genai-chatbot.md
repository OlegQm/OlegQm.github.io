## Building an AI application

At the Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava, I contributed directly to the development of a university GenAI chatbot as part of the AI team. My work covered backend and frontend development, connecting language-model capabilities with an interface people could use.

## My contribution

- Contributed to backend development with Python and FastAPI.
- Worked on the Vue frontend and user-facing interactions.
- Worked with the project's language-model tooling and data technologies.

## Technology experience

The project involved FastAPI, Vue, LangChain, MongoDB, ChromaDB and Docker. It gave me practical experience developing a GenAI application across the stack and collaborating within a university team.

## Project context

This was a team project, developed through collaboration within the university AI team. My role combined hands-on backend work with frontend development and the practical integration of GenAI capabilities.
